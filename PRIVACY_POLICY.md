# Privacy Policy for DocPort

**Effective Date:** May 7, 2026

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
DocPort ("the Extension") is built with privacy as a core principle. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, document contents, or browsing data.**

All text extraction and processing are performed entirely locally on your device within your browser.

## 2. Google Accounts and Permissions
The Extension does **not** use Google OAuth, nor does it connect to the Google Drive API. It does not have access to your Google account or your Google Drive files outside of the specific document page you are actively viewing and interacting with.

## 3. Local Storage
The Extension may utilize your browser's local storage (e.g., `chrome.storage.local`) solely to save your user preferences. This data never leaves your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services. Your activity remains completely private and is not monitored by us or any external parties.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be updated on this page with a revised "Effective Date".

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or the extension's data practices, please contact the developer.
